# Accuse the wrong suspect and lose

## Take the case
Click the "Take the Case" button.
Verify the room heading "The Lounge" is visible.

## Collect three clues
Click the clue "Amsel Pharmacy Matchbook" and close the clue card. Use the room navigation to go to "The Bar", click the clue "Eddie's IOU" and close the clue card, then click the clue "Pharmacy Receipt" and close the clue card.
Verify the notebook counter reads "3 of 8 clues".

## Accuse Eddie Crane
Click "Make an Accusation", choose the suspect "Eddie Crane", then click "Confirm Accusation".
Verify the ending screen shows the heading "Wrong Suspect".
